# Buying Audit Checklist

Mark each item: seen OK / problem / could not check. Only items marked "problem" go into the report.

## 1. Landing / home page
- Can a stranger say what is sold in 5 seconds (headline, hero image)?
- Is there one clear main button ("এখনই কিনুন", "অর্ডার করুন")?
- Is the phone number, WhatsApp or Messenger visible without scrolling?
- Are popups, auto-playing sound or banners covering the page on first load?
- Do ad or Facebook-post visitors land on the exact product they saw (message match)?

## 2. Finding products
- Is the menu understandable? Are category names normal words?
- Does search exist, and does it work for a misspelling or a Bangla word?
- Can the buyer filter by price and sort?
- Are out-of-stock items hidden or clearly marked?

## 3. Product page
- Are there several clear photos, including real ones (not only studio or stolen images)?
- Is the **price** visible near the button, with discount shown honestly?
- Are size, colour, weight, material or specs clear? Is the size guide present for clothes?
- Is the delivery charge and delivery time shown here, before the cart?
- Is stock status shown?
- Is the Buy button big, easy to tap, and above the fold on mobile?
- Are there reviews or customer photos? Are they believable (names, dates, not all 5 stars)?
- Is the return or exchange rule stated near the button?

## 4. Cart
- Can quantity be changed and items removed easily?
- Is the total, including delivery, clear before the next step?
- Is there a surprise charge or a forced account creation?
- Is a coupon box too prominent so that buyers leave to hunt for codes?

## 5. Checkout
- **Guest checkout** possible (no forced registration)?
- How many fields? Needed: name, phone, address. Anything beyond that (email, postcode, birth date) should be optional.
- Is the address form designed for Bangladesh (division / district / area, or one simple address box)?
- Is a valid-phone check present, and is the number input showing a number keypad on mobile?
- Are error messages clear and next to the field? Does the form keep what was typed after an error?
- **Payment options:** Cash on Delivery, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, card. Which exist? Is bKash/Nagad number or merchant flow clearly explained step by step?
- Is the delivery charge shown for the chosen area before confirming?
- Is there a clear order summary and a clear final button name?
- Is a confirmation (SMS, page, phone call) promised after ordering?

## 6. Trust and risk
- Real address, phone, trade licence number or Facebook page link present?
- Contact page works; policies (return, refund, privacy, terms) exist and are readable?
- HTTPS padlock present; no browser warnings?
- Does the site look abandoned (old dates, broken images, dead links)?
- Is there proof the shop is real: customer photos, delivery photos, video, social following that matches?
- Is the Cash on Delivery or "open box / check before pay" option offered where trust is low?

## 7. Mobile
- Text readable without zoom (about 16px or more)?
- Buttons and links at least 44px high and not too close together?
- Does anything need sideways scrolling?
- Does the sticky bar or chat widget cover the Buy button?
- Do images and pages load fast on a slow 4G connection (note any heavy hero video or huge images)?

## 8. Speed and technical basics
- Does the first view appear in about 3 seconds? Is there a layout jump while loading?
- Any broken images, 404 pages, console errors that visibly break a feature?
- Does the Facebook page link or WhatsApp button open correctly?

## 8b. Custom / personalised products (upload photo, add text)
- Does the editor page show the **price** and what the buyer will pay now (advance) and later?
- Are delivery charge, delivery time and the "no cancellation after confirmation" rule shown **before** the buyer finishes designing, not only on a separate page?
- Are file rules clear (type, size limit) and is it clear the photo is kept private?
- Is there a simple preview that matches what will be printed? Is technical wording (inches, margins, "safe area") explained in plain Bangla?
- Is "Add to cart" visible without a long scroll on mobile (sticky button)? Are secondary actions (save, download) readable?
- Is there a step where the shop shows the final design for approval before printing, and is that promised on the page?
- Is there proof of past work: sample photos, customer photos, reviews?

## 9. Facebook-page shops (no website)
If the "site" is a Facebook page or Instagram shop, check instead:
- Is the order method obvious (one pinned post, a clear number or Messenger link)?
- Are price and delivery charge in every product post, not only "inbox"?
- Are replies fast, and does the page show response time?
- Is there a pinned post with return and payment (bKash/Nagad) rules?
- Are real customer reviews and delivery proof posted?
- Suggest moving repeat buyers to a simple order form page.

## Severity guide
- **উচ্চ (high):** stops the order (no payment option the buyer uses, forced signup, hidden charges, broken checkout, no sign the shop is real).
- **মাঝারি (medium):** slows or worries the buyer (unclear delivery time, small photos, slow pages).
- **কম (low):** polish (spacing, wording, small visual issues).
